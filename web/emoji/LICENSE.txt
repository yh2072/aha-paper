Flat emoji artwork in this folder is Twemoji.

Copyright Twitter, Inc and other contributors; maintained at
https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji (v15.1.0).

Graphics licensed under CC-BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

They are served locally rather than from a CDN so that an exported offline
bundle keeps its icons with no network, and are used instead of the platform
emoji font, whose glyphs are glossy and three-dimensional on macOS.
